How is this related to animal path finding?

Again a video on Youtube, because who read those scientific paper work, right? (Actually do read them).

Some key points here:

Path integration is a mechanism used by animals that allows them to track their position and navigate in the environment. It's essentially an internal compass.

They process in the hippocampus the path finding by 2 elements

  1. Medical EC that have structural info, where am I?

  2. Lateral EC that have sensory, or cues, what am I seeing that related to my destination?

Combine these together they figure out where they are in space and which turn to take, this is actually on how they build an AI, we will get more on that in later work.

Seeing the pattern yet?

The above mean when an agent discover it way through space, it continuously collect cues and data that help prediction of what next, where to go.

Understanding the underlying mechanism of space or symbolism help this, knowing the pattern is knowing what is next. But thing is agent won’t know this at the beginning.

Like animal, any agent will have to learn to extract pattern from the underlying structure, to construct the space. Which have the same process as strategic thinking.

What were before are what will be next.

History repeat itself, whether it navigation in space or strategic thinking, the essential should be the ability to realizing the pattern, internalize then extract insights for prediction and innovation (A fancy word for combining things to make new things).

Beside that curiosity is another crucial characteristics, but that in the data collecting phase previously. I actually have a longer take on this, but for now this should be sufficient to give you an idea, to think about.
