The story of the Indochine’s elites

July 31, 2026 3 min read Your Name

Lately I found a video, an incredible one

This was about a Vietnamese family, through all the turmoil of wars, space and time, they still thrive.

Without social media today the world would have been very different, where everyone live their own life as we should be (Big question mark on if that was indeed the way life should be) and know nothing about others, or do we?

The story of the Indochine’s elites

It begins with the grandma, Lam Ngoc Thanh, who lived a memorable life. She was one of the founders of Cao Dai, a religion that has over 5 million followers in a 90 million-people country (2009). Imagine creating a religion that lasts for nearly 100 years and counting. She had 2 husbands, both high ranking officers and had a daughter.

Her daughter was proposed by Bao Dai, Vietnam’s last emperor; she rejected Bao Dai and then married a son of French Indochina - Laos Governor at that time.

In the end of French Indochina in Vietnam, they fled the country to France and lived a lavish life. They bring every treasure (That wasn’t supposed to be theirs, that was from a monarchy/colonial regime) without any plan. Later on, her daughters (Lam Ngoc Thanh’s grand-daughters) came to Bangkok and started a new life, married to a wealthy man, and still prosper today.

The thing is

This is a story that, from a historical POV, is really interesting. This family encapsulates French, Vietnamese, Laos history in the 1900 - 1945 Indochina, from an elite standpoint and still survive through everything.

I just wonder how many other stories like this, people find a way, not a very good way but still. They weren’t exactly living a life in secret, but without Youtube, I (we) would hardly know the details (Still not knowing the details to be exact).

Do you have friends but barely know what their family does? Do you know people that never show what they do for a living, but having a life that many would die for? I know several.

We think we know the world but barely know each other in a sense, that’s how we perceive famous people’s life, they show what they want you to consume, and still, you think that you know them well.

That’s just how it work.

Under the shade

How many others have their life that way because there are things to be hidden, treasures to be protected and statuses to be upheld.

I live my life in a shadow, the way I grew up and the only way I know how.

I like Monet paintings, especially this, this is Lady Monet and her son, strolling on a windy summer's day, in a sense we know who she is, but still far off from “commoner” eyes, that virtually at any moment, they would disappear behind the rolling hills without a trace.

Under that shade tree of the life we built, we live our own life far from the eyes of others, that’s the dream!
