The letter from me to you

Hey it’s Chris speaking

I always start my introduction like this, since it’s me coming up to you, being sincere and all that.

This would be a letter to all who want to get into tech with no prior experience or even someone that think they absolutely know what they want for the longest time, then dream do shatter and you do have another plan for life (Not the other way around).

The letter from me to you

Dear <How you would like to be called?>,

Growing up, I always knew that I wanted to work in Politics (Where everyone wear suit with that dead cold look). I pursued this dream and worked for several years in the field, even went further as going to another far away city, or later went for my first master in Diplomacy. But, there is always a big but (that bug me), something always felt missing.

I wanted a better world, but the world can hardly be the way I intended.

Everything happen for a reason, I got lost and found at the same time. Gotta ask “What is better, to be born knowing everything or go through life to figure thing out.“ Kinda similar to what Skyrim’s Paarthurnax said (Hardcore gamer reporting for duty here)

I found Tech (Where everyone wear T-shirt and sneaker).

First day, then first year, I still felt like a complete outsider. I had no prior experience in the field, and I felt like Icarus who flight too high. But as I immersed myself in the work, something clicked.

I discovered that I had a passion, something was burning here and there, now and then. Being all that hand-on, and I found myself thriving.

Looking back on my journey, I realize that stepping into tech as an outsider wasn't easy. But it was worth it. It’s take me almost 3 years, from Account manager in a tech company (Since I think it similar to my Diplomacy work as Partnership management/ External Relations, boy I was dumb), to Project manager (Meet a mentor here, kudos to him), to Product Manager (Great company here).

What a twisted run! But just keep running Forrest.

Here are some pieces of advice that I would give to anyone who is considering stepping into the tech industry without prior experience, or doing thing that other consider crazy in general:

  1. Be curious: You are dumb, truly dumb. So just ask for anything, you are that dumb.

  2. Be willing to take risks: Don't be afraid to take chances and try new things. Steve Jobs called Hewlett Packard (Yeah, it’s the HP stand for) when he was young asking for thing, just do those thing, no one care if you fail, but awe when you make it.

  3. Be adaptable: “Be like water, my friend” , Bruce Lee do you no wrong in this. Learn quickly, make decisions on the fly, and pivot when necessary.

  4. Be persistent: I got reject more often than success, much much more, so forget the 1xx CV you sent, send another hundred and more. You will be there one day.

I'm grateful for the opportunities that working in tech has given me, the one who taught and the one who listened, and I'm excited to see where this will take me next.

Whether you're an outsider like me or a lost soul, I hope that my story can inspire you to take risks, keep learning, and strive to be your best self. Be 10x not just 10%, go for it.

Sincerely,
Christopher

P/s: Didn’t know I need a name until I hear user struggle to pronounce mine, it was from Christopher Robin in Winnie the Pooh, I remember that boy somehow, and it just fit right in.
