Hello, World — Why I Started This Blog

July 29, 2026 3 min read Your Name

Every blog starts with a first post. It's almost a rite of passage — the moment you decide to carve out a small piece of the internet and say: this is mine. This is that post.

Why now?

For years I kept ideas in notes apps, half-finished drafts in Notion, and thoughts scattered across bookmarks I'd never revisit. Writing for social media felt hollow — the format demanded brevity, the algorithm demanded engagement, and neither of those things have anything to do with thinking clearly.

A blog is different. It's slow. It rewards finishing a thought rather than starting one. And it creates a record — something I can link back to, update, and be held accountable to.

What I'll write about

Honestly, I don't want to over-promise. The honest answer is: whatever I find genuinely interesting. That'll probably include:

  • Technology and how I use it day-to-day
  • Design — interfaces, systems, visual thinking
  • Books, ideas, and things I keep coming back to
  • Occasional notes and photos (shorter, looser)
"Writing is thinking. To write well is to think clearly. That's why it's so hard." — David McCullough

The format

Posts will vary in length. Some will be proper essays. Others will be quick notes — a single observation, a quote I liked, a photo I took. I've added a dedicated Notes section for the shorter stuff.

I'm not committing to a schedule. When I have something worth saying, I'll write it.

Subscribe if you want

If you want to be notified when new posts go up, there's a newsletter — link at the bottom of every page. No spam. I'm not collecting your data for anything other than sending you an email when a new post is live.

Thanks for being here. More soon.

— Your Name, July 2026
