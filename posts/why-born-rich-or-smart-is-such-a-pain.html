Why born rich or smart is such a pain?

July 31, 2026 5 min read Your Name

Why born rich or smart is such a pain?

Look at Instagram for fun or LinkedIn for business, being rich or smart early in life usually put a people on a pedestal of success faster, sooner. We are constantly bombarded with images of successful people who have achieved great things at a young age.

This can make it seem like being average is a bad thing. However, in real-life, I do believe that being average early in life can actually be a blessing.

The inherit generation

Many people I know work they whole life to give it to their child, but they have reasons for that, being born into a warzone they want the next generations should have a perfect life, thing they never even dreamt about when they were kids.

We don’t have trust-fund kid in my country, I know my rich people, something like Audi/Mercedes driving, million dollars house in Vietnam in their 20s, I would call them the inherit-everything-kid, that spend your entire yearly income in one birthday party before they turn 18 (Trust fund kid need to wait after 18).

It has huge perks, you don’t have to worry about life much, just where the next trip would be or which “haute couture” item(s) that you’re going to get this weekend.

Downside is, their lives are usually pre-defined, which mean you can predict pretty accurate on how their next 10 - 20 years would look like. They have it easy and keep it that way within their secret rich-people-network.

Weird thing is when you have limitless opportunities like that, you’ll also have limited options to actually do, because the “normal” stuff is too normal and you don’t want that, irony.

I was considered myself lucky, have enough to glance into the life of those people, and also need to work for a living so I have thing to do, a lot more thing to do.

And the smart kids

Born smart is also a privilege, I hunt smart people too, those weird breed and how to find them was something I love to do since I want to know things.

But the more I know the more I realize, if you are top-of-the-world-smart, like Television-news-interview-at-10 smart, they will go really far and unpredictable. Don’t know any of these in real life, yet!

But if you are national-prized on some particular thing or regional smart, you’ll most of the case be average good. I actually know 5 people like this, four with national Gold/Silver medals in French, Math, Badminton, Chess and one other, who have the reputation for being smart in my home town, where he won many awards. These kids were 10-15 years old at their peak.

The shocking thing is they are all average good now, meaning definitely not bad but they will do the exact thing normal person would do, might be with better companies, postitions but still the same thing.

The common pattern here is they reached their peak very early in life and then just stop. It’s just that, somewhere, somehow, they just feel like that is enough and stop trying, at least that was they said.

I’ve never won any awards, didn’t perform well academically and a disaster with any sport, ask my PE teacher, he detested me for failing a 70cm high rope-jumping that year.

But I’m still working with the mentioned-above-people in the same room.

If not wealth or intelligent, what is important?

For me it’s G-R-I-T – the ability to persevere in the face of challenges. It is the quality that allows people to achieve great things, under any circumstance. You can be born smart or rich but not with grit, it was not given, it should be hardened through life.

If I have to choose a person between bring born with advantages/have some success but casually gave up and someone who fail a lot but still there, standing strong and ready for more. I would always choose the latter, 101 out of 100 time.

Looking back to the best people I know, people that I won’t mention their name here, since you can actually Google them for real, they reached the peak in their industry, have great archievements. Being true success cases, these people all train (both mind and body) daily even in their 50s 60s. They all have absolute grit and taking nothing for granted since they came from zero.

The actual word by word in the conversation with one of them is: “We came this far from being a beggar, our family used to have nothing and we won’t lose it at this time”. She wasn’t that smart or rich to begin with I assured you, but she was smart enough, clever enough and most importantly, she tried to do better until to the end, even reached the goal for the last 20 years, she was still afraid of losing everything.

Do it!

For the early years in life, I’ve made terrible mistakes, I cocky and gave up on working, I instead day-dreaming, wasting my days under the sun, and life hit me in the face, dreaming alone won’t get you anywhere.

Later on, if you know me in real-life you would know my story, I realized that I was never the fastest, the strongest, or the smartest, so I build my tenacity up. I’ll be there for one thing only, because I’m a dumb, stubborn kid that just don’t quit.

So keep your dream big, but make sure you go for it all the way, I quote Marco P. White here - he has quite a personality - who actually had 3 Michelin stars then return it (Learned that yesterday!)
